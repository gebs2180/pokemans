1. Each card you post is yours to sell, genuine, and the card you listed, as its photos show it. You tell us about anything the photos don't show, such as a crease, writing, or a trimmed or altered card.
2. While a card is posted you don't sell it anywhere else. You can withdraw it until a buyer accepts. Once a buyer accepts, the sale is firm at the offer price for both of you, whatever grade the card later gets.
3. You ship the card within 3 days of its prepaid label showing in the app, with that label, in a sleeve and toploader in a bubble mailer, and say so in the app. Don't ship before the label shows. If you can't ship, tell us and we cancel the deal.
4. The buyer pays you the offer price directly, off the app, within 7 days of the card arriving as shown, using the PayPal, Venmo or Zelle details you give us. Keep those details right: we pass them on and don't check them, and we never handle the money. Tell us in the app when you've been paid, or if you haven't. They can send the card back only if it isn't genuine, isn't the listed card, or doesn't match its photos.
5. If a parcel is lost or damaged on the way, we claim on the label's insurance and pass what the insurer pays to you. The buyer owes nothing for a card that didn't arrive as shown.
6. Buyers see the photos of the cards you post. We store your photos, your cards' details and what happens to them (the offer, the sale, and any grade a card later gets), and use them to build and check our condition models and offers, including commercially. We may send the photos to pre-grading services we use, under their terms. We never sell your photos.
7. After a sale each side sees the other's name but not their email, and the buyer sees your payment details. They use them only for that sale. For anything else, contact us. Buyers never see your address, except as the return address on the parcel. During the test you don't sell to a buyer you met here outside the app.
8. We are not a party to the sale and don't hold cards or money. If buyer and seller disagree about whether a card matches its photos, Zach decides from the photos and what each of you says. We can void an offer or cancel a deal that went wrong; each is recorded with who, when and why. A card sent back as not genuine gets no new offer until we've reviewed it.
9. This is a test. Offers can be wrong and the app can stop working or be paused. Offers go up to $5,000, the most a shipping label can insure. Breaking these terms can end your part in the test.
