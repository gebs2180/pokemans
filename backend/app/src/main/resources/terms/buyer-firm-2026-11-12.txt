By accepting an instant offer here you agree that:

1. Accepting is a commitment to buy the card at the offer price. The price is fixed: it doesn't change with the grade you think the card is when it arrives, or the grade it later gets.
2. You pay the seller the offer price directly, off the app, using the payment details we show you once you accept, within 7 days of the card arriving, if it is genuine, is the card listed and matches the photos you saw when you accepted. We never handle the money. Otherwise you send it back within those 7 days, tracked and at your cost, and record why. Those are the only reasons to send a card back.
3. A card marked "not yet authenticated" is offered subject to authentication in hand: check it when it arrives, and send it back as not genuine if it isn't.
4. We buy an insured, prepaid shipping label for the card when you accept, from the seller to the address you gave us, and bill you for it (postage and insurance) each month. If we cancel a deal before the card arrives, its label isn't billed to you.
5. If a parcel is lost or damaged on the way, you owe nothing for it. We claim on the label's insurance and pass what the insurer pays to the seller.
6. You record in the app when the card arrives, then what you paid, or why you sent it back; "not genuine" needs a note saying why, and the seller sees it. The seller tells us whether they were paid. We keep these records and use them to check and improve our offers.
7. After a sale each side sees the other's name but not their email, and you see the seller's payment details. Use them only for that sale. For anything else, contact us. During the test you don't buy from a seller you met here outside the app.
8. We are not a party to the sale and don't hold cards or money. If buyer and seller disagree about whether a card matches its photos, Zach decides from the photos and what each of you says. We can void an offer or cancel a deal that went wrong; each is recorded with who, when and why. A posted card can be taken by another buyer first.
9. This is a test. Offers can be wrong and the app can stop working or be paused. Not paying for, or not sending back, a card within 7 days of it arriving ends your part in the test, and we tell the seller.

Ask Zach if anything here is unclear.
