Method
Each raw card's condition is a probability distribution over Ungradable and grades 1 to 10, from its photos. Each grade is 
priced from sold comps of that exact grade (never asking prices), net of grading costs. FMV is the probability-weighted price. 
QSV is a 30-day sale after fees and a speed discount. FLV is a 60-day forced sale: the distribution shifted two grades down 
(three at confidence C), after fees, a volatility shock (2 sigma over two months) and a market-depth discount. FLV is never 
above QSV. Cards at confidence D, with an unresolved authenticity flag, without verified custody, or under $25 FMV are 
excluded. Thin data lowers both value and confidence; nothing is interpolated upward.
Traceability
The grades each card's values rest on: probability (p), gross and net price, how the price was set, and the sold comps behind it. Grades below the lowest 
likely grade are shown where the stress reaches them.
