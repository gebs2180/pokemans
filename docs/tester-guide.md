# How the Rawmark test works

Thanks for testing. Rawmark gives you one firm price for a raw card, worked out from the grade it's likely to get. If a buyer takes it, you get a prepaid shipping label. This page is the short version. The terms you accept in the app are what count, so read them too, and ask Zach if anything is unclear.

## It's a test

- Offers go up to **$5,000**, the most a shipping label can insure. Cards worth more won't get one.
- Offers can be wrong, and the app can stop working or be paused.
- **There are no notifications yet.** Nobody gets an email when a card is posted, a buyer takes it or a label is ready. Open the app to check, and watch the Discord.
- During the test, don't buy from or sell to someone you met here outside the app.

## Getting set up

1. Open the invite link Zach sends you, sign in, and accept the terms.
2. Add your address: your ship-from address if you sell, your ship-to address if you buy.
3. To photograph a card, take a front and a back photo of each card, plus a close-up if the app asks for one. **If Android asks to let Chrome take pictures, tap "While using the app".**

## Selling a card

- **One firm price.** Once your card is identified and checked, you get one price. "How we priced it" shows how we got there. An offer is good for 7 days.
- **Sell** puts your card in front of every buyer for 14 days. Posting isn't a sale yet: you can withdraw it until a buyer takes it. While it's posted, don't sell it anywhere else.
- **The price is final.** Once a buyer takes it, the sale is firm for both of you at that price, whatever grade the card gets later. The buyer won't re-grade it on arrival and come back with a lower offer. They get the upside if it's a 10, and the risk if it isn't.
- **Shipping.** You don't pay postage: the buyer does. Wait until the prepaid label shows on the card page, then ship **within 3 days**, with that label, the card in a **sleeve and toploader inside a bubble mailer**. Don't ship before the label shows. If you can't ship, tell Zach and he'll cancel the deal.
- **Getting paid.** The buyer pays you the offer price directly (not through the app) **within 7 days of the card arriving** as shown.
- **Lost or damaged in the post?** We claim on the label's insurance and pass on what the insurer pays.

## Buying a card

- **Cards for sale** shows the photos each price was based on. The first buyer to accept gets the card at that price.
- **Accepting means you're buying.** The price doesn't change with the grade you think it is when it arrives, or the grade it later gets.
- **Postage is yours.** We buy an insured label when you accept and bill you for postage and insurance once a month.
- **When it arrives,** record it in **Your deals** and pay the seller within **7 days**.
- **You can send a card back for only three reasons:**
  1. it isn't genuine;
  2. it isn't the card that was listed;
  3. it doesn't match its photos.

  Send it back within those 7 days, tracked and at your cost, and record why in the app. "I think it's an 8, not a 9" isn't a reason: condition the photos show is your risk.
- A card marked **"not yet authenticated"** is sold subject to you checking it in hand. If it isn't genuine, send it back.
- Not paying for a card or sending it back within 7 days ends your part in the test.

## If something goes wrong

If buyer and seller disagree about whether a card matches its photos, Zach decides from the photos and what each of you says. He can also withdraw an offer or cancel a deal that went wrong, and the app shows you why. We never hold your cards or your money.
