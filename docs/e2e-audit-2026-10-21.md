# E2E audit before the Discord beta

2026-10-21 · main at `86c6ed6` (margin by confidence merged; PRs #23 and #24 not yet merged) · audit only, no code changed. Statuses after the fixes are in "Status after the fixes" below the top 10.

The whole instant-offer flow was clicked through in the running app, as admin, collector and buyer, at 375×812 (phone) and 1024×768 (laptop): upload, identify, review, offer, sell, buy, label, arrival. It **works end to end only after faking extra comps**. With realistic hand-entered comps, no card gets an offer (finding 1).

## How it was run

- **Stack:** a separate Postgres and MinIO on ports 5442/9010 (compose project `pokemans-e2e-audit`, database `pokemans_e2e`). Backend `bootRun` on 8091, Vite on 5183. Zach's 8080/5173 and his `pokemans` database weren't touched. Everything was stopped and the audit volumes removed afterwards.
- **Login:** `docs/local-dev.md` has no dev-auth or test-user path, so a throwaway mock OIDC issuer ran from a scratch folder outside the repo. It served the Auth0 endpoints that `auth0-spa-js` and Spring use, and minted tokens for test users: admin with the ADMIN role and the MFA claim, a collector and a buyer. The issuer and audience were set through the usual env vars, so the app's code ran unchanged.
- **Providers:** there are no runtime fake pre-grade or EasyPost providers. `FakeShippingProvider` exists only in tests. Pre-grading, card reading and EasyPost were left off, so condition was entered by hand and every label purchase failed with "no shipping account". TCGdex was live.
- **Photos:** the composited test cards in `Pictures\pokemans-test-cards`. The browser pane can't open a file picker, so photos were put into the page's own file inputs from script. The upload, the photo checks and the storage path were all the real ones.
- **Data:** the real sold comps from `comps.csv` for Umbreon VMAX 215/203, Charizard Base 4/102 and Pikachu VMAX 188/185. Pikachu then got extra **fake** comps (marketplace "E2E FAKE", URLs on `example.test`) to get past finding 1.

Not covered: a real or test EasyPost label (no key), printing a label, withdrawing a listing, two buyers racing (covered by `OffersFlowTest`), expiry, contributor and graded-card flows, a high-risk card's offer (Charizard never got one), a real phone camera, and the actual Auth0 sign-up and MFA.

## 1. No offer for any card with realistic comps · Blocker

**Steps**
1. As admin, identify Umbreon VMAX 215/203 and enter the 9 real comps from `comps.csv` (3 each at PSA 10, 9, 8). Refresh prices.
2. Save a distribution: 7 10%, 8 35%, 9 40%, 10 15%, confidence B.
3. As the collector, open the card and tap **Get an offer**.

**Result:** `409 There isn't enough sales data to make a firm offer on this card yet.` The same happens for Charizard (8 comps) and Pikachu (8 comps).

**Why** (`OfferService.price` refuses confidence D; `CardValuator.confidence`):
- comps confidence C, since the probability-weighted count of observed sales is 2.7 and `compsForB = 5`;
- minus one level for the fallback σ: with fresh hand-entered comps there are never enough 30-day changes to measure σ, so every card gets the penalty;
- minus one more because the stressed distribution reaches derived grades. Nobody enters comps for PSA 5–7, so they are always derived.

So C → D → no offer. Pikachu reached an offer ($100.54, confidence B) only after 33 fake comps: 11 each at PSA 8, 9 and 10 (weighted count ≥ 10, so A), plus 3 each at PSA 5, 6 and 7, so no derived grade is in play. That's about 40 comps per card, by hand.

**Screenshot:** the "Instant offer" box on a phone. The **Get an offer** button, with red text under it: "There isn't enough sales data to make a firm offer on this card yet." There's nothing else in the box.

**For Zach to decide:** this is the methodology working as designed ("thin data lowers value *and* confidence"), but it means the beta has no offers unless comps go in at that volume. Options: a CSV import of comps (finding 23), a σ fallback that doesn't also cost a level for the offer use, or an explicit beta rule. It's a parameter or methodology call, not a bug fix.

## 2. Labels: none without EasyPost, and the copy says otherwise · Blocker for a real ship

**Steps:** with `EASYPOST_ENABLED=false`, the buyer buys Pikachu.

**Result:**
- The buyer sees: "Bought. The seller's label didn't go through yet (No shipping account is set up yet, so the label will follow.); try it again from your deals." The punctuation is broken, and "will follow" contradicts "try it again".
- **Try the label again** on Your deals returns the same message, forever.
- The seller's card page says **"Your prepaid label is on its way."**, with no hint that the purchase failed.
- The admin "Offer track record" counts the deal as "1 · 0 labelled · 1 on the way".
- There's no label to print anywhere, so "print the label" couldn't be tested.

**Expected:** before the beta, an EasyPost test key so the label path runs once (the checklist already lists this). Until then, the seller should be told the label is delayed and that we'll be in touch, not that it's on its way.

**Screenshot:** the seller's card page on a phone. A green "Sold to E2E Card Shop … They pay you $100.54 once it arrives as shown." with grey "Your prepaid label is on its way." under it.

## 3. Photos stay editable after the sale, and nothing checks they show the card · High

**Steps**
1. Sell Pikachu and have the buyer accept.
2. As the collector, open the sold card. **Retake** is still there for the front, back and close-up.
3. Upload the Lugia V front as Pikachu's front.

**Result:** "Passed · 1536×2048, 4 attempts, all kept." The upload is accepted after the sale. The only image check is exact duplicates: re-uploading Umbreon's front gave "This exact image was already uploaded for another card." Earlier, a back photo uploaded into the **Front** slot also passed.

**Why it matters:** the buyer terms let a card go back when it "doesn't match its photos". If the photos can change after acceptance, neither side can prove which ones were shown.

**Expected:** freeze capture once a card is listed, or at least once it's sold. Show the photos as they were at acceptance. A front/back sanity check would catch the swapped slot.

## 4. The market shows all photo attempts, superseded ones included · High

**Steps:** as above, the collector uploads the back into the Front slot by mistake and retakes the front. Then they sell, and the buyer opens **Cards for sale**.

**Result:** three images: a back, the mistaken "front" (also a back), and the real front. They have no captions. The alt texts are "back", "front", "front", and only screen readers see them. The admin review page shows the same strip ("back · card crop", "front · card crop", "front · card crop"), with no current or superseded marker, back first.

**Screenshot:** a 375 px market card. "Pikachu VMAX / Vivid Voltage #188, EN, holo", then two identical blue Poké Ball card backs side by side, the rainbow Pikachu front wrapped onto a second row, then "$100.54".

**Expected:** show only the current front and back, labelled, with the close-up if there is one.

## 5. Arrival can be recorded before shipping, and below the firm price · High

**Steps:** the buyer opens **Your deals** straight after buying, before any label exists. The form shows "Arrived as shown, paid", already selected, with **Paid ($)** prefilled at 100.54. Change it to 95 and tap **Record**.

**Result:**
- Accepted: "Arrived; you recorded paying $95.00." The "No label yet. Try the label again" line stays above it.
- The seller's page then reads, all at once: "They pay you $100.54 once it arrives as shown." / "Your prepaid label is on its way." / "Arrived. They recorded paying $95.00."
- The seller has no way to dispute the amount, and the buyer wasn't asked why they paid less.

**Also:**
- One radio button records two different events, arrival and payment, although the terms give 7 days between them.
- When "Sending it back" is chosen, the reason defaults to "doesn't match its photos", the vaguest of the three, which will skew the return data.
- The deal page shows no photos of the card, so the buyer can't check "matches its photos" from it.

**Expected:** allow arrival only once a label exists, or with an admin override. Record payment separately from arrival. Require a note when the amount paid differs from the offer. Default to no return reason. Show the listed photos on the deal.

## 6. Low grades priced near zero from the card's own step-down · High

**Steps:** enter the real Charizard Base 4/102 comps (2 × PSA 10, 3 × PSA 9, 3 × PSA 8) and refresh.

**Result:** the steepest adjacent ratio, 9/10 = 0.130, is applied all the way down: PSA 7 $185.87, 6 $24.24, 5 $3.16, 4 $0.41, 1 $0.00. A raw vintage Charizard with a median of 6 would get an offer of a few dollars, or none under the $25 minimum. Real PSA 5–6 Base Charizards sell for hundreds. Umbreon (ratio 0.547) and Pikachu (0.587) look more plausible, but those are steep too.

**Why:** it's the methodology's rule (the card's own steepest per-grade ratio until era ratios exist). For vintage, the 10/9 gap is a scarcity premium, not a per-grade step.

**Expected:** a beta rule. Either require comps at the grades the distribution covers before offering, or don't derive from the 10/9 ratio. If testers see a $3 Charizard offer, they'll stop trusting the prices.

## 7. The admin can't see offer readiness, and has no label or deal tools · High

- **No offer preview on the review page.** The page shows the price table (confidence per grade) and the distribution, but not the confidence the offer will get or whether it will be refused. Finding 1 was only found by reading `OfferService` and `CardValuator`.
- **No admin UI for labels.** `POST /api/admin/deals/{id}/label` exists but nothing in `web/src` calls it. `offers.md` says "the buyer or the admin buys the label again".
- **No admin list of listings or deals.** The only views are the aggregate "Offer track record" and "Postage to bill". The latter showed "No labels that month." because no label existed.
- The price table footer says "parameters 1.0.0", while offers are priced with 1.2.0. Both are right (pricing and valuation have separate sets), but it reads like a mismatch.

PR #24 (admin undo and a maximum offer) may cover part of this. It wasn't merged when this audit ran.

## 8. Collector dead ends and missing status · Medium

- **After accepting the invite**, the org page shows only "Collections / New collection name / Create / No collections yet." There's no hint that the path is a collection, then a card, then photos, then waiting for review, then an offer.
- **Get an offer** shows before the card is identified, and tapping it gives a red "We haven't identified this card yet." It doesn't say who does that, how long it takes, or that nobody will tell them (no notifications, as the checklist notes).
- **The collection list** only ever says "Photos OK", even once a card is identified, priced, listed or sold.
- **The card page** keeps the collector's typed name, never the confirmed catalog identity. The Authenticity box uses internal wording ("Not in an authenticity-risk category.").
- **The home page** is just "Your orgs". Reaching a card takes three taps (org, collection, card), and there's no "my cards" or "my sales" view.
- **Add card** stays on the list, so you have to tap the new card to photograph it.

**Screenshot:** a phone, org page. A large "E2E Collector Zach-test" heading, "Collections", an empty text box with a Create button, "No collections yet.", and white space below.

## 9. Identity search can lead to the wrong set · Medium

**Steps:** on the review page for the Charizard, search "Charizard" with number "4/102".

**Result:** "Charizard · base4 4", "Charizard · base1 4", "Dark Charizard · base5 4", "Charizard δ · ex14 4". The `/102` is ignored, results show set codes rather than set names, and Base Set 2 is listed first. Thumbnails help, but Base and Base Set 2 Charizards look almost the same. Picking `base1` then shows "Charizard · Base Set 4 · Rare 1999", so the set name appears only after the click. The collector's typed name isn't prefilled into the search.

**Expected:** show the set name and total in results ("Base Set · 4/102"), filter or rank by the denominator, and prefill from the collector's text.

## 10. Stale Authenticity panels on the review page · Medium

**Steps:** on a card that isn't identified yet, confirm its identity. Then use the browser's back button and open the next card from the queue.

**Result:** after **Confirm identity**, the page has two Authenticity sections. The old one says "Not identified yet: no offer until it is. Confirm the card's identity first…", directly above the new one ("High-risk card, not yet verified…" for Charizard). After moving to the next card in the app, a third, stale Authenticity section showed **above** the "← Review queue" link. A full reload clears them.

**Screenshot:** a phone, admin review page for Pikachu. At the very top, before the back link and title, a bordered box: "Authenticity / Not identified yet: no offer until it is."

## Everything else

### Capture (collector)
11. **Medium. The camera may be forced on phones.** The photo inputs have `capture="environment"`. On Android Chrome this usually opens the camera directly, so a collector can't pick a photo they already took (from a lightbox setup, say). Worth a check on a real Android phone.
12. **Medium. No photo preview.** After an upload the card page shows only "Passed · 1536×2048", so the collector can't see what they uploaded. Combined with finding 3, that's how a back in the Front slot goes unnoticed.
13. **Low. Contradictory status.** After a rejected retake, the page header says "All required photos passed." while the Front box says "Retake needed: This exact image was already uploaded for another card."
14. **Low. Lots checkbox.** "Lot of cheaper cards" appears on every collection, and lots never get offers. Its accessible name is "on".
15. **Low. A "Loading…" flash.** It shows on card and review pages after navigating within the app.

### Selling
16. **Medium. Selling takes one tap.** "Sell for $100.54" posts at once, with no "you're committing to ship if a buyer accepts" step. Buyers get a two-step **Buy**, **Confirm: buy for $X**. **Withdraw** is also a single link-styled tap (not exercised).
17. **Medium. The ship-from address is hidden.** Once saved, it isn't shown on the card page and can't be changed there. The buyer's org page shows its address with **Change**.
18. **Low. Address form.**
    - **State** needs a two-letter code, but its placeholder is just "State". "Illinois" turns into "ILLINOIS" and the browser says "Please match the requested format."
    - There are no `autocomplete` attributes, so phone autofill won't help.
    - **ZIP** has no numeric keypad (`inputmode`).
19. **Low. "How we priced it" leaves things out.** It's clear and the numbers check out by hand: per grade (gross × 0.98 − $32) × 0.85 × 0.90 × 0.87, weighted to $100.54. But it never mentions that grading cost is deducted, which is why a PSA 10 "worth" $160.68 sits well below the $279 comps.

### Buying
20. **Medium. The missing-address error has no link.** Buying without a ship-to address shows "Add the address cards should ship to first." at the top of the market list, which is off-screen on a phone after tapping Buy further down, and it doesn't link to the org page where the address is entered.
21. **Low. Thin market listing.** It shows "Vivid Voltage #188, EN, holo", with no total (/185), rarity or "subject to authentication" example (no high-risk card got an offer to check).
22. **Low. Seller contact details.** After buying, the buyer sees the seller org name and email (fine for a test), but not the ship-from city, so they can't estimate delivery.

### Admin
23. **Medium. No bulk comp entry.** Comps go in one at a time; the form usefully keeps grader, grade and date between saves. `comps.csv` already exists for 5 cards, about 9 rows each. Finding 1 needs about 40 per card, so a CSV import matters for the beta.
24. **Low. The review queue doesn't show the org or collector**, and uses the full page width while other pages are narrow.
25. **Low. The reviewer can set confidence A with no close-up on a modern full art.** The methodology's default downward surface shift isn't prompted.
26. **Low. The distribution grade inputs have no individual labels** beyond the visible grade number (fine visually).
27. **Low. Defaults on a modern card.** Confirming identity defaults Edition to "unlimited" even for modern cards. Harmless, but noise.

### Everywhere
28. **Low. The header can overflow on phones.** At 375 px the email and **Log out** share the top bar. "e2e-collector@example.test" fits, but a longer address would wrap or overflow.
29. **Low. The logged-out invite page doesn't mention the invite.** Opening an invite link while logged out shows the generic landing ("Instant offers on raw cards." / **Log in**). After login it does return to the invite, which then says "Join E2E Collector Zach-test / You have been invited as a collector."
30. **Low. Placeholder truncated.** "Card, e.g. Charizard Base Set Unlimited 4/102" is cut off at 375 px.
31. **Low. Admin org type defaults to Partner.** The offers beta needs Collector and Buyer orgs.

### Worked well
- Login returning to the invite, both invite accept pages, and the buyer terms page. It's plain-language and ends with "Ask Zach if anything here is unclear."
- Photo checks pass the composited cards and catch duplicate images across cards.
- The comp form's validation (grade 1–10 in halves), and the price table's per-grade "How" column.
- The offer math, the 13% margin explanation, the two-step Buy, and the buyer's address with **Change**.
- No horizontal scroll on any page tested at 375 px.
- The offer track record's caveats and "too few to read" labels.

## Suggested order before inviting testers
1. Decide finding 1 (and 6) with the methodology: the beta has no offers without it.
2. Get an EasyPost test key and run one label through (finding 2). Fix the seller copy either way.
3. Freeze photos once a card is listed, and show only current photos (findings 3 and 4).
4. Gate arrival behind a label, and split arrival from payment (finding 5).
5. Give the admin an offer preview and a label retry (finding 7), and a comps CSV import (finding 23).
6. Then the collector status and next-step copy (finding 8), the identify search (9) and the stale panels (10).
