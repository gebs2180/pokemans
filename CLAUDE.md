# pokemans — raw collection collateral reports

**Product name:** Rawmark (`rawmark.app`, chosen 2026-10-25). "pokemans" stays the internal name in packages, Gradle, databases, buckets and Auth0 identifiers.

Collateral reports for raw-heavy Pokémon card collections: what a collection recovers in a forced sale, with stated confidence, so a lender can advance against it. Also replacement values for insurers. We don't lend, hold cards, or set advance rates.

**Source of truth:** the *Raw Collection Valuation Methodology* Claude Doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/1XVmPmLtueJk37u2hXgDmL). This file summarizes it as of 2026-10-18. If they disagree, the doc wins: re-read it and update this file.

**System design:** [docs/hld.md](docs/hld.md) covers the architecture, modules, data model, flows and milestones. Read it before adding modules or entities.

## Non-negotiable principles

- **Condition is a distribution, not a grade.** Each raw card gets probabilities over 11 outcomes: Ungradable (U), 1…10. Uncertainty widens the haircut instead of being hidden.
- **Four values, always:** FMV, RV (replacement), QSV (30-day quick sale), FLV (60-day forced liquidation). Insurers cover on RV; lenders advance on FLV.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Every price traces to *sold* comps with dates and counts. Never use asking prices.
- **Portfolio, not cards.** Concentration, liquidity and correlation are first-class.
- **Conservative by default.** Thin data lowers value *and* confidence. Never interpolate or shift upward, whether for prices, grades or photo confidence.
- **Store everything,** including wrong predictions. Photos, model/API predictions, our distribution, human adjustments and returned grades are all calibration and training data.

## Pipeline

1. **Identify & inventory.** Per-card front and back photos for every card at or above $25 est. raw value; bulk lots aren't supported (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc). Photos are timestamped and kept (they double as pre-loss proof for insurance). Canonical ID: set, number, language, edition (1st Ed / Shadowless / Unlimited), print variant, promo status → catalog ID. Authenticity-risk categories (vintage holos, high-value JP promos, recent chase) get flagged, and **flagged = zero collateral value** until verified. Verifying a flag needs an in-hand inspection, a grader's slab or third-party authentication. High-risk cards (vintage holos and 1st Editions, Japanese promos) can't be verified from photos. A recent chase card can be verified by a photo review. The review names the photos it used, which must include a passing close-up. A finding, verification or doubt, counts only for the identity it was made against, so re-identifying a card resets it (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc). Record custody (who/where/since); unverified custody = no advance. Partners and collectors record their own custody, but only an admin verifies it, and a check lapses after 90 days (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc).
2. **Condition distribution.** A photo model scores centering, corners, edges and surface, and a human reviewer adjusts early on (each adjustment is training data). Half grades are always folded down, until half-grade comps exist to price them (Zach, 2026-11-04; not yet in the methodology doc). Low photo confidence shifts probability *down*. A single provider is labelled confidence B but moves no probability down for that alone; providers more than a grade apart (B) and photos not showing the whole card (C) still do (Zach, 2026-11-10; not yet in the methodology doc). When the provider's lowest of corners, edges and surface, folded down, is at or above its overall grade, its grade gets a tighter spread (+1 10%, 0 60%, −1 25%, −2 5%); a lower subgrade or none given keeps the default spread, and the provider's centering subgrade is never read (Zach, 2026-11-10; not yet in the methodology doc). There is no default surface shift for holos or full arts: surface comes from the pre-grade providers' grades, or our own pre-grading once we have it (Zach, 2026-11-04; not yet in the methodology doc). Our own centering measurement caps a system distribution at the best grade PSA's table allows and is never averaged in; a later "not measured" leaves the cap in place, side by side, so a retake that measures only the back keeps the last front's limit. A reviewer's distribution above the cap is refused unless they override it, which is recorded with their reason. Another provider's centering is never used (Zach, 2026-10-31; side by side and the reviewer rule 2026-11-01; not yet in the methodology doc). An assessment counts only for the front and back it was made from. Once either is retaken, the card gets no offer until it is assessed again ("Ask again" or a reviewer). The centering cap carries over (Zach, 2026-11-04; not yet in the methodology doc).
3. **Per-grade prices.** Sold comps of that exact grade, 90-day window, recency-weighted (30-day half-life); extend to 180 days if fewer than 2 sales. Price = recency-weighted median, outlier-trimmed when n ≥ 10.
   - PokeTrace (live source since 2026-11-07, written permission for offers, display and keeping the data) gives daily rows with a deliberately noised count, so each day with a sale is one comp at the day's average and the count is never used. Counts are sale days, a lower bound on the sales. An admin confirms every card mapping; one that disagrees with our card needs a reason (Zach, 2026-11-07; not yet in the methodology doc). From parameter set 1.5.0 a PokeTrace day is left out of pricing, never shifted, when its average is outside its own low–high, its high is over 3× its low, or its average is over 3× the median of at least 2 other sale days of its tier within 90 days; only high days are caught, and each one is stored with its reason (deep review B4, 2026-11-09; thresholds are defaults to confirm; not yet in the methodology doc). See [docs/poketrace-pilot.md](docs/poketrace-pilot.md).
   - Missing grade, sales on both sides → interpolate on log price.
   - Sales only above → higher grade × era step-down ratio (median adjacent-grade price ratio across comparable same-era cards). With no era ratio yet, use the card's own steepest per-grade ratio if it has sales in at least 2 grades at least 2 apart; otherwise leave it unpriced (Zach, 2026-10-17). The step up to grade 10 is left out of that steepest ratio, since a PSA 10 is its own market like a PSA 1; grade 10 still counts towards the 2 grades and the spread, and a card whose only step ends at 10 stays unpriced (Zach, 2026-11-09; parameter set 1.4.0; not yet in the methodology doc).
   - Sales only below → use the lower grade's price unchanged. **Never step up.**
   - Grade 1 is priced from its own sales only and never anchors another grade: PSA 1s have their own collectors and often sell above 2–4 (Zach, 2026-10-17). So there is no era 1-to-2 ratio either: a grade 1 without its own sales borrows the era's 2-to-3 ratio, else the card's own ladder (review, 2026-10-17; Zach confirmed 2026-10-18).
   - Any derived price → confidence drops one level.
   - Each grading company (PSA, CGC, BGS, …) is priced separately from its own comps, never pooled or converted. A raw card is valued on PSA's table only: no PSA table means unpriced and excluded, with no fallback to another grader (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc).
   - U priced from damaged/altered raw sales, else $0. No bulk floor (Zach, 2026-10-17). Low-value cards are excluded by the $25 FMV minimum instead.
   - Each grade's price is **net** of grading fee, shipping, insurance and a time-to-cash discount, but use the raw-sale price for that condition if it's higher. The grading cost of the day is an input to each valuation run, starting at $25 fee, $5 shipping, $2 insurance per card and 2% time-to-cash (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc).
   - A price table no refresh has replaced (sales aged out or purged) is still used, at lower confidence: one level once it is more than 7 days old, one more every 30 days after, down to D (Zach, 2026-10-18).
   - Volatility σ = std dev of 30-day price changes over 12 months, stored per card, from graded sales only (never raw). With too few changes to measure it, a valuation uses a fallback σ of 20% a month and drops confidence one level (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc). From parameter set 1.5.0 a measured σ above 1.0 is treated the same way (deep review B4, 2026-11-09; cap is a default to confirm; not yet in the methodology doc).
4. **Values** (p = grade probability, P = net price):
   - `FMV_i = Σ_g p_ig · P_ig`
   - `RV_i = FMV_i · (1 + b + s_i)`, using the central (unstressed) distribution. b = 12% buyer costs; s = search premium of 0% (>20 sales/mo), 5% (5–20), 10% (<5).
   - `QSV_i = FMV_i · (1 − f) · (1 − d_i)`. f = 15% fees+shipping; d = 5% (>20 sales/mo), 10% (5–20), 20% (<5).
   - `FLV_i = FMV↓_i · (1 − f) · (1 − k·σ_i·√t) · (1 − ℓ_i)`. FMV↓ shifts the distribution 2 grades down (3 if confidence C), floored at grade 1, with U probability unchanged; each grade is priced at most the grade above it, so the shift can't gain from a PSA 1 premium. k = 2, t = 2 months, ℓ = market-depth discount (share of a month's volume to sell), floor 10%, cap 50%. FLV is never above QSV (Zach, 2026-10-18).
   - Confidence A–D from photo quality and comp counts. D is excluded from the collateral base.
   - A card whose returned grade's cert is verified is valued as graded: certain at that grade (half grades fold down), on its own grader's table at gross prices, with no grading cost and no condition stress shift; it counts in the graded share and is left out of grade-first (Zach, 2026-10-18; no condition stress confirmed 2026-10-19: a graded card is priced at its grade, whatever its condition; not yet in the methodology doc).
5. **Portfolio.** Eligible = not D, no unresolved auth flag, verified custody, FMV ≥ $25. Lots are never valued. Single card ≤ 20% and top five ≤ 50% of the eligible FLV before caps, applied by capping every card at one level, at every collection size with no minimum card count (Zach, 2026-10-19; replaces the 2026-10-18 final-base rule, which squeezed 10–15 card pledges down to their smallest card's FLV and had a cliff at 10 cards; not yet in the methodology doc). So one card counts at most 20% of its FLV, and five or fewer at most 50%; >40% of base in one set or character → extra 10% off that slice. A set is language plus set name. A character is the Pokédex species in any language, form or owner (Charizard ex, Dark and Mega Charizard and リザードン are one slice), and a tag team counts toward its more popular partner (Zach, 2026-10-19; not yet in the methodology doc). Report raw vs graded share. `Advance = α · Σ min(FLV_i, cap_i)`. α is the **lender's** choice: suggest a range (e.g. 50–70%), never set it.
6. **Grade-first list.** `Lift_i = (FLV_graded − FLV_raw − C_i) / C_i`, where C = fee + shipping + insurance + waiting cost. Recommend lift > 1.0 and show the top 20 with expected grade and cost. FLV_raw here is computed from gross prices, so grading cost counts once, in C (Zach, 2026-10-18; not yet in the methodology doc). Doubted cards are left off.
7. **Reports.** A valuation run stores every input and the full trace, and the reports render from the run alone. Lender report: collateral base, suggested α range (the lender sets α; a lender-given α adds one line), caps and haircuts with reasons, exclusions with reasons, raw vs graded share, confidence, grade-first top 20, traceability. Insurer report: RV for every identified, priced card with its photos as pre-loss proof; counterfeit or suspected cards get RV $0 with the reason; custody and confidence D don't exclude. Only the admin issues reports; members of the collection's org can download them (Zach, 2026-10-18).

8. **Instant offers** (Zach, 2026-10-19; the lender and insurer side is paused meanwhile). A collector gets **one firm price** for a raw card: `price = Σ p_g · offer_g`, where `offer_g = P↓_g · (1 − f) · (1 − d) · (1 − m)`, the net price capped at the grade above and m the buyer margin by the card's confidence: 10% at A, 13% at B, 17% at C, so a less certain condition leaves the buyer more room (Zach, 2026-10-21; parameter set 1.2.0; not yet in the methodology doc). The confidence an offer is priced at leaves out the penalties that only bear on FLV: a fallback σ, and derived prices that only the stress reaches. It keeps the photo and comps base, a stale table, and a derived price where the central distribution puts probability. Derived (extrapolated) grades still price offers (Zach, 2026-10-21; not yet in the methodology doc). The 10 rung is capped: `offer_10 = min(offer_10, M · offer_9)`, both after the net price, the cap at the grade above, f, d and m, with M = 2.5 (`offers.maxTenToNineMultiple`, default to confirm); offers only, so FMV, RV, QSV, FLV and the grade-first list keep the full ladder; a 9 rung of zero leaves the 10 alone, and the trace records when the cap binds (Zach, 2026-11-09; parameter set 1.4.0; not yet in the methodology doc). The buyer keeps the upside of a 10, up to that cap, and carries the downside; no grade is judged on arrival, so there is no back-and-forth. Selling posts it to every buyer org; the first to accept buys it at the price, and posting is not a sale. Offers last 7 days, listings 14. On accept, a prepaid label insured for the price is bought on our EasyPost account and billed to the buyer monthly. The buyer pays the seller off-platform within 7 days of a card arriving as shown, or returns it only if it's not genuine, not the listed card, or doesn't match its photos. Nobody buys their own card: a buyer org sharing a member with the seller's org is refused, to keep the track record honest. Recording a doubt voids the card's live offers and takes it off the market; a card already taken stays a deal for the admin (Zach, 2026-11-02; not yet in the methodology doc). No offer for lots, doubted cards, confidence D, FMV under $25, or a price above the maximum, EasyPost's $5,000 insurance limit, so every label is insured for the full price (`pokemans.offers.max-price`; the $500 beta maximum of 2026-10-21 was lifted 2026-10-26 because too many cards were above it; Zach; not yet in the methodology doc); unverified high-risk cards get one subject to authentication in hand. Defaults to confirm (deep review 2026-11-09, not Zach's decision yet): an offer below the card's lowest raw near-mint comp of the last 90 days is flagged to the admin and seller, never refused. At post and accept, the offer's ladder is re-run on today's prices, and a move of more than `pokemans.offers.reprice-threshold` (10%) either way voids it by appending rows and refuses with "The price has changed. Get a new offer." The admin undoes by appending, never editing: a voided offer comes off the market and can't be sold; a deal cancelled before arrival frees the card for a new offer, its label isn't billed to the buyer and can be marked for a refund; each is a new row with who, when and why (Zach, 2026-10-21; not yet in the methodology doc). See [docs/offers.md](docs/offers.md).

**Regression check (worked example, illustrative numbers):** FMV $411 → RV $481 → QSV $314 → stressed FMV $181 → FLV $107 → advance at α=60% $64. Inputs: ~10 sales/mo, σ = 8%, one copy (ℓ = 10%), f = 15%, b = 12%, s = 5%. Full grade table is in the doc; use it as an early unit test.

## Validation targets

- Condition: actual grade within ±1 of predicted median ≥ 80% of the time; below the stressed median < 5%. 1,000+ partner cards plus a 20–30 card controlled PSA set.
- Liquidation: replaying the 2021–2023 decline, FLV computed at peak covers price 60 days later for ≥ 95% of cards.
